SwipePlay Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated: 24 Nov 2026.

What we collect

Email address and password (for your account), a username, the text prompts you send to generate games, the games you create or publish, and your likes, reports and blocks. We also count how many games you generate per day to enforce a limit.

How we use it

To run your account, generate games, show published games to other users, and prevent abuse. Prompts are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate game code. Accounts, games and activity are stored in Supabase.

Sharing

Games you publish, with your username, are visible to other users. We do not sell personal data or use it for ads. Prompts are processed by Google Gemini. Data is hosted by Supabase.

User-made games

Games are made by users with AI and run in a restricted sandbox. You can report or block games and creators in the app. Reported games may be hidden or removed.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Account, Delete account) or on the account deletion page. Deletion removes your account, profile, games, likes, reports and blocks.

Children

SwipePlay is not meant for children under 13.
